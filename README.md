# Zomato Analysis Dashboard 🍕📊

A comprehensive business intelligence dashboard designed to evaluate food delivery, restaurant operations, and overall sales performance patterns. This repository contains the documentation and strategic layout for analyzing broader revenue trends, customer satisfaction drivers, geographical performance tiers, and operational inventory structures.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview & Strategic Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to model and visualize the complex relationships between restaurant outlet characteristics, customer feedback, and sales performance. By leveraging multi-dimensional filtering and granular category breakdowns, stakeholders can uncover structural insights into how location tiers, outlet formats, and product lines influence overall market success.

---

## 📈 Analytical Framework & Core Sections

### 1. Global Filtering & Dynamic Controls

* **Outlet Location Type Slicer:** Allows analysts to segment performance across distinct geographical market tiers, evaluating how urban versus suburban consumer behaviors shift business outcomes.


* **Outlet Size Slicer:** Enables comparative analysis across small, medium, and high-scale establishments to determine how physical store capacity correlates with revenue generation.


* **Item Type Slicer:** Provides deep-dive capabilities into specific product and food categories, helping decision-makers identify top-performing inventories and customer preferences.



### 2. Revenue & Sales Performance Dynamics

* **Geographical Tier Distribution:** Evaluates sales concentration across various market tiers, demonstrating how regional positioning impacts overall market penetration and revenue share.


* **Establishment Scale Analysis:** Examines how outlet sizing influences total commercial output, highlighting which store formats capture the highest volume of consumer transactions.


* **Longitudinal Growth Trends:** Traces historical performance trajectories across multiple establishment years, capturing multi-year revenue evolution and identifying peak operational milestones.



### 3. Customer Satisfaction & Product Quality Insights

* **Nutritional Content Impact:** Analyzes customer rating behaviors across different dietary categorizations (such as regular versus low-fat options) to assess whether product attributes influence consumer sentiment.


* **Cross-Dimensional Quality Mapping:** Evaluates how ratings fluctuate across combined dimensions, such as geographical tiers paired with specific product features.


* **Inventory Satisfaction Evaluation:** Measures consumer feedback across a diverse spectrum of item types, ranging from fresh foods and beverages to household goods.



### 4. Outlet Typology Evaluation

* **Format Efficiency:** Compares diverse structural business types—ranging from neighborhood grocery formats to large-scale supermarkets—to understand transaction volumes and operational throughput across different retail environments.



---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

* **Data Visualization Tool:** Power BI / Tableau (or equivalent interactive dashboard canvas)
* **Data Modeling:** Multi-table relational schemas mapping transactional sales records, categorical item hierarchies, and geographical outlet dimensions.

---

## 🚀 Usage Guide

1. Open the dashboard project file within your designated Business Intelligence workspace.
2. Utilize the interactive sidebar filters (Outlet Location Type, Outlet Size, and Item Type) to dynamically refresh all connected visual elements and isolate specific operational segments.


3. Hover over individual graphical data points to inspect detailed underlying breakdowns regarding temporal trends, category sentiment, and localized sales volumes.
